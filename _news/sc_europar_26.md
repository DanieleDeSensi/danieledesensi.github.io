🎉 I'm happy to announce that three papers have been accepted at SC 2026: *High-Performance Tensor Formulation of the Viterbi Algorithm for Hidden Semi-Markov Models* (Lorenzo Piarulli, Elia Belli, and myself), *Provisioning Networks for AI Supercomputers: A Trace-Driven Study of Performance Sensitivity at Unprecedented Scale* (in collaboration with ETH Zurich and Microsoft), *Characterizing the Scalability and Performance of Large-Scale AI Training Under Multi-Tenancy* (collaboration with University of Trento, NVIDIA, ENEA, and Julich). We also got two posters accepted at Euro-Par 2026: *Fairness Implications of Ultra Ethernet Congestion Control in Datacenters* (led by Taha Rahmani) and *Short Hops, Big Gains: Beyond Asymptotic Costs in Collective Communication on Dragonfly+* (led by Chiara Di Stefano). Congratulations to all the co-authors.
