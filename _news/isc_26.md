🎉 I'm happy to announce that two papers have been accepted at ISC 2026: *PICO: Performance Insights for Collective Operations, presents a tool for fine-grained profiling and benchmarking of collective operations* (work led by Saverio Pasqualoni) and *Characterizing the Impact of Congestion in Modern HPC Interconnects* (work led by Lorenzo Piarulli). Saverio and Lorenzo will be presenting their papers at ISC this June in Hamburg.
